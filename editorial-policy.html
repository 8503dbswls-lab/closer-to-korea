Editorial Policy

Closer to Korea explains products, routines, and trends through their real-life context in Korea. This policy describes how we separate firsthand experience, research, product identity, availability, and commercial relationships.

How products are selected

Products may be included because they appear in Korean daily life, are personally purchased or used, represent an established product category, or are receiving meaningful current attention. A product is not selected only because it looks Korean or is easy to monetize.

Match labels

  • Confirmed Match: The exact brand, product, model, or unique identifying information has been verified.
  • Likely Match: The product appears to match, but one or more model-level details remain unconfirmed.
  • Similar Alternative: A different product serves a similar use, form, or function and is not presented as the same item.
  • Korean Culture-Inspired Pick: The item is not the same product but is connected to Korean culture, daily life, or a relevant Korean trend.

Experience and research

Firsthand use, personal purchase, observation in Korea, published research, and retailer information are treated as separate forms of evidence. The site does not describe a product as personally used unless that experience is documented.

Availability and updates

Prices, inventory, shipping eligibility, packaging, and product listings can change. Product and purchase links are reviewed periodically, and the last-checked date is shown when available.

Commercial independence

Affiliate commissions and advertising do not change the stated product-match label or verification status. Commercial links are disclosed clearly and are added only when the destination and match status can be described accurately.

Corrections

Readers may report a factual error, outdated link, or unclear product match through the contact page. Material corrections are reviewed and updated as soon as reasonably possible.

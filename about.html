About the curator

Real Korean products need real context.

Closer to Korea is curated from a Korean perspective for U.S. readers who want to understand what people in Korea actually use, where products appear in daily life, and how confidently a product match can be identified.

Why this site exists

Korean products often travel online without the routines, environments, or cultural details that make them understandable. This site explains those missing details before linking to a product.

How products are selected

  • The product is genuinely used in Korean daily life.
  • It reflects a Korean routine, climate, home, work, school, food, beauty, or cultural context.
  • It is receiving current attention in Korea and that trend can be responsibly explained.
  • It is unfamiliar enough in the US to benefit from explanation.
  • When U.S. options are discussed, exact products and alternatives are labeled clearly rather than treated as the same item.

How evidence is labeled

Personally Used

Used only when the curator has directly used the product. This label is never inferred.

Seen in Korean Daily Life

Observed as part of ordinary Korean home, retail, work, school, transport, food, or beauty routines.

Trending in Korea

Used only when current attention can be reasonably supported without treating a niche as universal.

Research-Based Pick

Selected through research rather than personal product testing.

How product matches are labeled

Every product match uses one of four labels: Confirmed Match, Likely Match, Similar Alternative, or Korean Culture-Inspired Pick.

A similar use or appearance does not make two products the same item.

The label reflects how much product-level evidence is available and is kept separate from whether a U.S. purchase option exists.

How trends are reviewed

Trend products include a first-published date, a last-checked date, and a status such as Rising, Trending, Established, Everyday Standard, Fading, or Archive. Products that have not been reviewed recently can be marked Review Needed.

Affiliate links and independence

Closer to Korea may earn a commission from qualifying purchases. Affiliate eligibility does not determine whether a product is selected or how it is described. Price, stock, sellers, and product details can change.

Weekly updates

New products and guides are added as their facts, images, and Korean-life context are verified.

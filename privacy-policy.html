Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 16, 2026

This policy describes how Closer to Korea handles information on the current website. The site currently uses Google Analytics 4 for audience and site-usage measurement. This policy will be updated as the services used by the site change.

Google Analytics

Closer to Korea uses Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors find and use the website. Google Analytics may use first-party cookies, including the _ga cookie, and may process information such as pages viewed, referral source, device and browser information, approximate geographic information, and interaction events. Google states that Google Analytics 4 does not log or store individual IP addresses. Standard Analytics reports do not provide Closer to Korea with a visitor's name, email address, or other information that directly identifies that person.

Analytics information is used to understand aggregate traffic patterns, evaluate content performance, and improve the website. Visitors can use browser privacy controls or the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on to limit Analytics collection.

Current data collection

The site does not currently offer user accounts or a newsletter signup. Closer to Korea does not ask visitors to provide personal information simply to browse the site.

Hosting and security logs

The hosting and domain infrastructure may process technical information such as IP address, browser type, requested pages, timestamps, and security logs as part of operating and protecting the website.

Contact by email

If you contact Closer to Korea by email, the information you choose to send is handled through the relevant email service and used to respond to the inquiry or address the issue you reported.

External links

Pages may link to third-party websites for sources, references, or other relevant information. Those services follow their own privacy and data practices.

Children

The site is intended for a broad audience and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Advertising and affiliate services

The site does not currently run display advertising or active affiliate tracking. Before those services are activated, this policy and any consent or disclosure controls required for the services and the visitors concerned will be reviewed and updated.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the site or the services it uses change. The latest revision date will be shown on this page.

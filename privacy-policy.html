Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

This policy describes how Closer to Korea handles information on the current website. The site currently uses Google Analytics 4 for audience and site-usage measurement and Microsoft Clarity for website interaction analysis. This policy will be updated as the services used by the site change.

Google Analytics

Closer to Korea uses Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors find and use the website. Google Analytics may use first-party cookies, including the _ga cookie, and may process information such as pages viewed, referral source, device and browser information, approximate geographic information, and interaction events. Google states that Google Analytics 4 does not log or store individual IP addresses. Standard Analytics reports do not provide Closer to Korea with a visitor's name, email address, or other information that directly identifies that person.

Analytics information is used to understand aggregate traffic patterns, evaluate content performance, and improve the website. Visitors can use browser privacy controls or the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on to limit Analytics collection. Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services and read Google's Privacy Policy.

Microsoft Clarity

Closer to Korea uses Microsoft Clarity to understand how visitors interact with the website, such as page views, clicks, scrolling, and navigation patterns. Clarity may provide heatmaps and session-replay style reports that help identify usability issues and improve site design. Microsoft may process device, browser, approximate location, interaction, and technical information as part of this service.

Clarity may use first-party and third-party cookies, depending on consent signals, region, and service settings. Microsoft describes its cookie practices in the Clarity cookie documentation and its data practices in the Microsoft Privacy Statement. Closer to Korea does not use Clarity to intentionally collect form passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information.

Cookies and privacy choices

Cookies are small files stored by your browser. Analytics services currently load when pages are opened; the site does not currently provide an on-site cookie preference panel. Browser settings can block or delete cookies and may limit some analytics features. Deleting cookies does not delete information already processed by a service. The provider links above explain additional privacy controls.

Current data collection

The site does not currently offer user accounts or a newsletter signup. Closer to Korea does not ask visitors to provide personal information simply to browse the site.

Hosting and security logs

The hosting and domain infrastructure may process technical information such as IP address, browser type, requested pages, timestamps, and security logs as part of operating and protecting the website.

Contact by email

If you contact Closer to Korea by email, the information you choose to send is handled through the relevant email service and used to respond to the inquiry or address the issue you reported.

External links

Pages may link to third-party websites for sources, references, or other relevant information. Those services follow their own privacy and data practices.

Children

The site is intended for a broad audience and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Advertising and affiliate services

The site does not currently run display advertising or active affiliate tracking. Google AdSense is planned but is not active.

If AdSense is activated, third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to show ads based on earlier visits to this website or other websites. Google's advertising cookies allow Google and its partners to personalize ads using that browsing history. Ad services may also process IP addresses, device and browser information, and ad interactions to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud.

You can manage or opt out of Google's personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings. Google's partner-sites privacy information explains its use of data. These controls do not turn off all advertising or replace choices offered by a site's consent controls.

Before advertising or affiliate tracking is activated, this policy must reflect the actual providers and data practices, and the required consent and disclosure controls must be configured and tested. For personalized AdSense ads shown to visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, Google requires a Google-certified consent management platform that integrates with the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework. This site does not currently operate that advertising consent platform. Any future consent controls must explain how to change or withdraw consent and identify the participating providers.

See our Advertising Disclosure and Affiliate Disclosure for more about commercial content.

Privacy questions

For questions about this policy or a request concerning information you have sent us, email contact@closertokorea.com. Please do not include passwords, payment details, or identity documents.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the site or the services it uses change. The latest revision date will be shown on this page.

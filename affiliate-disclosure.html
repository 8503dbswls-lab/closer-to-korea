Commercial transparency

Affiliate Disclosure

Closer to Korea may use affiliate links in the future. When an affiliate relationship is active, eligible links will be identified clearly and the relationship will not change the price paid by the reader.

Current status

No active affiliate purchase links are currently published on Closer to Korea.

Where disclosures appear

If affiliate links are activated, a plain-language paid-link disclosure will appear close to the relevant link or call to action rather than relying only on this page.

Product-match labels

Product identity and commercial availability are separate judgments. Matches are labeled as Confirmed Match, Likely Match, Similar Alternative, or Korean Culture-Inspired Pick.

Editorial independence

Whether a product can generate a commission does not determine whether it is covered, how it is described, or which match label it receives.

Prices and availability

Retail prices, sellers, shipping, stock, and product specifications can change. Current values will not be stated as fixed unless they have been checked using an appropriate source.

Images and trademarks

Retailer images, trademarks, prices, and listing information will be used only in ways permitted by the relevant program rules and rights holders.

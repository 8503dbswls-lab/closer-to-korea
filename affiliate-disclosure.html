Commercial transparency

Amazon affiliate disclosure

Closer to Korea may earn a commission from qualifying purchases made through clearly identified Amazon links. This does not change the price paid by the reader.

Where disclosures appear

A plain-language paid-link disclosure appears near an active affiliate CTA. A legal page alone is not used as the only notice.

Exact products and alternatives

Listings are marked as Exact Korean Product, Similar Product Available in the US, Korean-Style Alternative, or Inspired by a Korean Trend. When the Amazon listing is not the same Korean item, the page states:

This is not the exact Korean listing, but it is the closest widely available alternative I found on Amazon US.

Prices and availability

Amazon prices, sellers, shipping, stock, and product specifications can change. The site does not present a fixed price as current unless a checked date and compliant data source are available.

Editorial independence

Commission eligibility does not determine whether a product is covered or recommended. Korean context, usefulness, evidence, product identity, and US availability are evaluated separately.

Image and trademark use

Product images, Amazon marks, prices, and listing information must be used only in ways permitted by the applicable Amazon Associates agreement and brand guidelines.

Current prototype status

The supplied prototype contains no active Amazon Associates links. Buttons remain disabled until the exact destination, product match, disclosure, and tracking information are verified.

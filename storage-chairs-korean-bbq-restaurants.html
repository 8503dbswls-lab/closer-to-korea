What Are the Storage Chairs at Some Korean BBQ Restaurants?

At some Korean BBQ restaurants, the chair is more than a place to sit. It can also provide space for a coat or bag.

Published 2026-08-19Updated 2026-08-20

A Chair That Can Hold Your Coat or Bag

At some Korean BBQ restaurants in Korea, you may find chairs with storage space inside the chair or beneath the seat. That space can be used for coats and bags, giving diners somewhere to put their belongings while they eat.

Why the Korea-Based Editor Found It Useful at a Grilling Restaurant

The Korea-based editor found this storage useful as a practical place to keep coats and bags tucked away while dining. In that local observation, the storage chair is an optional setup seen at some Korean BBQ restaurants rather than a feature found at every restaurant.

Not Every Restaurant Has One

The setup varies by restaurant. If you visit a Korean BBQ restaurant and do not see a storage chair, the absence of one does not mean you are missing a standard feature. In the editor’s local observation, this type of chair can be a useful option at some Korean BBQ restaurants rather than a standard feature across Korean dining.

Final Takeaway

If you see a chair with storage space at a Korean BBQ restaurant, it may be the place for your coat or bag. The seating also provides a practical storage option.
